How to escape

Space / Up / W / tap to jump. Hold to repeat. P pauses. Crashes cost a heart. Answer a CPU question to escape the bug. Wrong answers explain the concept and offer one recovery question. Reach the finish of each level to unlock the next stop on the map. Heart refills appear at 500 m, 1,100 m, then every 600 m.
