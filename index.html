CSC3501 / CIRCUIT RUNNER

Escape the CPU.

Run through a motherboard, leap over transistors, and climb floating chip platforms. Stay on the circuit as your speed increases.

  • Jump: press Space, up arrow, W, or tap. Hold to repeat jumps. Press P to pause.
  • Survive: hitting an obstacle or falling into a gap breaks a heart. Answer the CPU question correctly to revive; a wrong answer ends the round.
  • Manage your hearts: start with three. If you crash with none left, the round ends.
  • Find refills: when a heart is missing, look for pickups at 500 m, 1,100 m, 1,700 m, and every 600 m after. Each restores one heart, up to three.
